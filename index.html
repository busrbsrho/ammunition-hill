WHAT THE MODEL IS BASED ON

Sources & accuracy

These are two research-based interpretations. No verified 3D scan or complete measured survey was found. The modern view is partial and is not a verified September 2026 survey.

June 1967

Main trench centerlines and building positions are hand-traced from the museum-attributed battle map. Small firing bays are simplified; underground rooms are not reconstructed.

The modern memorial

Building footprints, paths and site outline use OpenStreetMap data retrieved on 26 September 2026. The western trench is an approximate interpretation of the historic route. The covered eastern trench is not shown as intact. Individual trees, exact trench survival boundaries and current underground interiors are not modeled.

Battle replay & landmark evidence

The replay presents documented phases, not a record of every soldier’s movement. Dashed paths and tokens are schematic; tokens do not represent troop numbers. Events overlap, and accounts disagree on some times. Playback uses equal-length phases for reading, not elapsed battle time. Hover cards identify uncertain placements. Minefield boundaries and underground interiors are not reconstructed.

What would make it more precise?

A measured site survey or photogrammetric scan, dated aerial photographs, trench cross-sections, and archival building plans checked against what was actually built. The soldiers’ routes illustrate their documented actions; no personal route is surveyed.
