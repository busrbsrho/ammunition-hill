THE PEOPLE OF THE HILL

Eitan Naveh

איתן נאוה

1944–1967 · Paratrooper

His life

Born on 1 June 1944 in Moledet, Eitan volunteered for the paratroopers. He served as a reservist during the Six-Day War.

His contribution to the battle

On 6 June 1967, he left the trench and fired his machine gun from exposed ground, covering his comrades and enabling them to advance. He was killed while providing that cover, aged 23.

MEDAL OF VALORAwarded posthumously in 1973

What would make it more precise?

A measured site survey or photogrammetric scan, dated aerial photographs, trench cross-sections, and archival building plans checked against what was actually built. Eitan’s marker indicates the western-trench area; it is not an exact surveyed point.
